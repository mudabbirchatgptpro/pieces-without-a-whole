## E1 Sequencing / scheduling evidence
Recommended article level:
- "Newsela provides the majority of its content at five different reading levels so students have the greatest possible opportunity for growth, then continuously calculates customized article levels for each student and:" followed by "Presents students with articles at that level by default." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656274-student-article-level, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Rather than a diagnostic, a student’s article level is a recommendation of the best level for the articles they will read next." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656274-student-article-level, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Article Level Recommendation is a recommended level per article, not overall for the student. i.e. it is not an overall placement predictor." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656274-student-article-level, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "When students do not yet have performance data within Newsela, and MAP Growth data is imported into Newsela, students automatically see all content at the Lexile level closest to their MAP Growth scores on both assignments and independently selected texts." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656240-nwea-and-newsela, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Newsela’s Reading Skills Checks provide grade-level content to help your students establish a recommended Article Level in Newsela more quickly." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656039-reading-skills-checks, accessed 2026-10-24)
Assignment remains teacher-selected:
- "Supplies the recommended level as a reference to teachers who can set the article level of assignments at the recommended level or at a specific level of their choosing." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656274-student-article-level, accessed 2026-10-24)
Scope: level adjustment applies to Newsela texts; no documentation found of Newsela sequencing activities from other vendors' tools.

Grade passback:
- "Since Newsela is an adaptive tool that allows students to take quizzes multiple times and adjust article levels, individual scores graded in Newsela do not pass back to the Canvas SpeedGrader." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656010-canvas-for-system-admins, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Student quiz scores, annotations, and writing prompt response submissions can be viewed and manually graded in the Canvas SpeedGrader." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656010-canvas-for-system-admins, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Since Newsela is an adaptive tool which allows students to adjust lexile levels take and take quizzes multiple times, individual Newsela quiz score do not pass back to Schoology." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656009-schoology-for-system-admins, accessed 2026-10-24)
Rostering standards:
- "For districts with a Newsela subscription, Newsela will sync all data shared in Classlink on a nightly basis." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656002-classlink-for-system-admins, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "In order to connect NWEA MAP Growth scores with Newsela, districts must set up a Clever or OneRoster connection." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656240-nwea-and-newsela, accessed 2026-10-24)

## E3 Learner model evidence
- "Newsela’s recommended article levels for students are determined based on student performance data on Newsela’s quizzes and other activities." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656274-student-article-level, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Newsela allows districts to sync NWEA MAP Growth data to help us recommend initial article levels for students." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656240-nwea-and-newsela, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "However, as students begin engaging with Newsela content and quizzes, their performance data from within Newsela will carry more weight into their recommended article level than the NWEA data." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656240-nwea-and-newsela, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Each student’s MAP Growth-based reading level will be visible in the Student Overview report, with an indicator that shows this value was informed by MAP Growth data." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656240-nwea-and-newsela, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Newsela ELA multiple choice quizzes are aligned to your state standards, based on the location of the school in which you're registered." and "Teachers are able to view indicators of student performance across these standards in their Classroom Data report." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656317-standards-alignment, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Each level of every Newsela article has its own 4-question quiz connected to reading skills." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13928499-classroom-data-report, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Formative results stay separate: "When you assign an activity on Formative, those results will be visible from within Formative at this time. When teachers assign an article on Newsela, those results will only be visible within Newsela at this time." (https://help.newsela.com/en/articles/13656189-formative-x-newsela-integration, accessed 2026-10-24)
