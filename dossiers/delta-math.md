## E1 Sequencing / scheduling evidence
Teacher-built assignments:
- "At DeltaMath, we believe that the teacher is the ultimate judge of completion and rigor. Using the rigor settings of \"required\" and \"penalty,\" you can tailor assignments to your students' needs and your desired outcomes." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/assignments/assignment-skill-settings, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "DeltaMath offers 2500+ skills for lower elementary through 12th grade that you can use when creating assignments. Almost every skill has a bank of 100 questions, which are served randomly to students upon assignment." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/assignments/add-skills-to-an-assignment, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "DeltaMath allows teachers to mix and match problem-sets, control rigor, vary due dates, and, with PLUS or INTEGRAL, create tests and problems of their own." (https://www.deltamath.com/, accessed 2026-10-24)
Automatically generated remediation (own content):
- "DeltaMath test corrections serve as targeted remediation assignments. Individualized test corrections can be generated from either a DeltaMath test or another classroom-based assessment." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/test-corrections/introduction-to-test-corrections, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Linked test corrections are derived from tests created in DeltaMath. Student test scores and skills are automatically retrieved based on their test performance. Subsequently, an individualized remediation assignment is generated for each student." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/test-corrections/introduction-to-test-corrections, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Test corrections unlinked to DeltaMath are derived from tests or other assessments not generated within DeltaMath. Students manually input their test scores, and teachers must choose DeltaMath skills that align with their classroom-based assessments." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/test-corrections/introduction-to-test-corrections, accessed 2026-10-24)
Within-skill hints:
- "Typically, there are multiple steps to complete, and if a student submits an incorrect intermediate step, a “notice” will appear with a hint about what to do next." (https://help.deltamath.com/en_US/getting-started-gc/content-overview-gc, accessed 2026-10-24)
