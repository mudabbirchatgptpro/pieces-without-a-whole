## E3 Learner model evidence
- "For every blueprint standard a student has been assessed on, the platform computes a mastery score from that student's results." (https://support.derivita.com/understanding-the-readiness-score, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Quizzes and exams only. Mastery is built exclusively from scored assessments. Homework and practice are shown for context but do not count toward the score." (https://support.derivita.com/understanding-the-readiness-score, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Recent work counts most. When a student is assessed on the same standard more than once, the most recent attempt is weighted most heavily" (https://support.derivita.com/understanding-the-readiness-score, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Shows Readiness = attainment × coverage (mastery of what's been taught, scaled by how much of the blueprint has been taught so far)." (https://support.derivita.com/readiness-vs-mastery, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "The bands are the same everywhere: Mastery 80-100, Near Mastery 65-80, Emerging 0-65." (https://support.derivita.com/teacher-standards, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Real-time visibility across cohorts, schools, courses, and students, down to standards and individual items." (https://www.derivita.com/, accessed 2026-10-24)
Data flowing out to LMS:
- "Scores and usage data flow back to the LMS automatically." (https://www.derivita.com/lms-integrated-math-platforms, accessed 2026-10-24)
Outside vendors' tools writing mastery or skill data into Derivita: none found on the pages listed in this dossier.
