## E1 Sequencing / scheduling evidence
- "Allows teachers to assign homework and assessments for the entire class or a select group of students" (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/introduction.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Offers progress monitoring assessments for an adaptive testing experience" (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/introduction.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "There are five Progression Benchmark Tests, one per domain. Each test has an entry point based on the student’s course (or other teacher-specified entry point) and adapts based on student responses to the questions to assess student understanding of the objectives within that domain across varying grade levels." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/how-does-the-progression-benchmark-work.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Assignments are automatically scored, and students have access to immediate remediation on homework questions." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/resources.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "An Assignment Guide and Homework Check has been provided for every section as well. Teachers can easily determine which exercises they should assign to which levels of learners, whether they are basic, average, or advanced." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/differentiation.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- No documentation found of sequencing across other vendors' tools.

## E3 Learner model evidence
- "Teachers can only view individual students in the Progression Benchmark report. Teachers see each student’s progress related to each domain and grade level. By selecting one of the domains, teachers can view the subdomains within that domain. Teachers can then view the specific objectives the student was tested on within the subdomain, and if that student answered correctly or incorrectly." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/What-do-teachers-see-in-the-Progression-Benchmark-report.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Teachers see a percentage indicating how students performed on the objectives covered on each benchmark, so teachers can easily see growth throughout the year." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/benchmark-standards-report.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Teachers may review a variety of reports with information including standards, remediation, and assignment performance." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/reports.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Teachers can also assign the Progression Benchmark test, which is an adaptive test that assesses students on a continuum of skills." (https://help.cengage.com/bim/teacher/bim/resources.html, accessed 2026-10-24)
- No documentation found of outside vendors' tools writing data into these reports.
