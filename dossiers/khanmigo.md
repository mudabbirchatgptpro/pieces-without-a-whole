## E3 Learner model evidence
- "Khan Academy's Mastery System helps you track your learning progress in a course. As you practice, your skill levels can change based on your performance, helping you see which skills you've learned and which ones you may want to continue practicing." (https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/115002552631-What-are-Course-and-Unit-Mastery, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "As you practice a skill and answer related questions, you can move through different skill levels: Not started: You haven't started working on the skill yet. Attempted: You've started working on the skill. Familiar: You've demonstrated some understanding of the skill. Proficient: You've demonstrated proficiency in the skill. Mastered: You've demonstrated mastery of the skill. Your skill level can move up or down based on your performance as you continue practicing." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Your mastery percentage shows the percentage of skills in a course or unit that you've reached Proficient or Mastered on." (https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/48411237993357-How-is-my-mastery-percentage-calculated, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "To increase your mastery percentage, level up more skills to Proficient or Mastered by completing activities such as: Practice exercises Quizzes Unit tests Course Challenges" (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Khanmigo teacher tool "Class Snapshot Gives you a high-level analysis of your students' work over the past 7 days, including their learning time, assignment completion data, course mastery progress, and more!" (https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/14799047733645-What-teacher-tools-are-available-on-Khanmigo, accessed 2026-10-24)
- What leaves Khan Academy for the LMS, per the vendor: "assignments, progress, and completion data" (https://blog.khanacademy.org/a-sneak-peek-at-whats-coming-next-for-khan-academy-districts, accessed 2026-10-24). No page opened describes skill-level or mastery data being sent to another vendor's system, or other vendors' tools writing into Khan Academy's mastery system.
- "Improved filters and export options make it easier to analyze progress across courses, including Khanmigo AI activity in a single export." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)

## E4 Data protection evidence
- "This guide is a technical resource for districts who are already Khan Academy Districts partners and are preparing to share their data with Khan Academy." (https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/17100737312397-Khan-Academy-Districts-Clever-set-up-guide, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "The additional data requirements for students are: district, name, school_id, sis_id, student_id, date of birth, email, and grade. Student date of birth: Khan Academy has two levels of permissions for student accounts under FERPA and NY Section 2-d regulations. Students under 13 have limited permissions on discussion posts and computer science projects." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "If the changes you make to your Clever or ClassLink roster are more than 50% of the existing totals on Khan Academy, the roster change will not go through and a member of the Khan Academy team will be in contact with you to ensure those changes are intended." (https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360031158952-How-does-automated-rostering-work-for-Khan-Academy-Districts-accounts, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Khanmigo simplifies your workflow while keeping your work and your student data private and secure." (https://www.khanmigo.ai/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "we are only offering access to learners who are under 18 years old when their parents give them access or when their school district is partnered with Khan Academy." (https://www.khanmigo.ai/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Khan Academy is itself an app that receives data from rostering layers; no page opened describes Khan Academy granting per-app permissions to other tools.
