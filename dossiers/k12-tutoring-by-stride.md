# Dossier: K12 Tutoring by Stride (Stride, Inc.)
Frame stratum: 5.4   Evidence collected: 2026-10-24   Collector: stratum-5.4 agent D
Frame source: Instructure, The Edtech Top 40: K-12 Edtech Engagement During the 2025-26 School Year, entry 'K12 Tutoring by Stride' (list is alphabetical, unranked)

Note on the Canvas-facing product: the K12 Tutoring help center documents one Canvas-facing component, "Beacon by K12 Tutoring" (an AI math assistant), which students reach from Canvas through a sign-in link. Tutoring sessions themselves run in Engageli (see E5).

## E4 Data protection evidence
- "After accepting the invitation, your district’s Clever administrator must configure the required data-sharing permissions." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/k12-tutoring/district-admins/clever-integration-setup-single-sign-on/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Your Clever sharing rules should match the terms of your K12 Tutoring agreement to ensure that only authorized students’ data is shared." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/k12-tutoring/district-admins/clever-integration-setup-single-sign-on/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Student conversations and activity are only shared with approved educators supporting the student’s learning experience." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/beacon/beacon-faqs/about-beacon-by-k12-tutoring/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Self-initiated Homework Help and Skill Sessions appear in the student’s session history, but their transcripts are only viewable by the student, not by educators since they were not educator-assigned." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/beacon/educators-beacon/hw-help-skills/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Make sure you are logged in with educator-level permissions." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/beacon/educators-beacon/using-the-insights-tab/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Redirect Tool isn’t available. Your institution’s Canvas admin may have restricted external apps." (https://faq.tutoring.k12.com/beacon/educators-beacon/adding-beacon-to-your-canvas-course/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "IMPORTANT: Please direct all requests related to FERPA-covered education records and course data directly to the public school in which the student is enrolled." (https://stridelearning.com/privacy-policy/, accessed 2026-10-24)
