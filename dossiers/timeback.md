## E1 Sequencing / scheduling evidence
- PowerPath API tag: "Course Sequence ... Manage course sequences and query course sequence to get the next stage in the student learning path" (OpenAPI embedded in https://docs.timeback.com/beta/api-reference/beyond-ai/powerpath/course-sequence/get-a-single-course-sequence-by-id.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Stage types in the course sequence schema: "enum: - course - assessment - hole-filling ... description: The type of the stage" (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "learningAppId: ... Only for Hole-Filling stages. The id of the learning app that should be used for the hole-filling course. examples: - alpha_write - mobymax" (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "A convenience endpoint that gives back the `learningAppId` of the hole-filling stage by the `grade` and the `subject`." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/api-reference/beyond-ai/powerpath/course-sequence/get-a-hole-filling-app-id.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Supported remediation workflows can use assessment results and missed standards to select targeted content and schedule a retest. Eligibility, score thresholds, grade bands, available content, and progression steps vary by subject, app, and workflow. A score below 90% does not universally or immediately create a hole-filling course." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/how-it-works.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "For developers, hole filling means your content participates in a system that actually responds to failure. If students struggle with specific lessons, the platform can route them to remediation (from your app or another) and bring them back to retry. An adapter or configured workflow must support the required content and routing." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Vendor caveat on that page: "This is a conceptual map. Available launchers, dashboards, analytics, and remediation flows depend on the deployed product and configuration. The SDK and API guides describe the verified integration contracts; the diagrams do not establish a live deployment or universal automation." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Level 2 integration: "Learning engine | Use [PowerPath] for sequencing and mastery logic" and "Level 2 apps use Timeback's learning systems instead of building their own. You read Timeback's placement data and act on it." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/build-on-timeback/integration-levels.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Level 1 integration: "You keep your own: Learning engine and algorithms (e.g. no PowerPath) ... Placement and diagnostic logic" (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Students launch learning apps through the Timeback Desktop App." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/how-it-works.md, accessed 2026-10-24)

## E3 Learner model evidence
- "The middle layer provides **mastery tracking and the closed-loop feedback system**. It takes raw events from apps and turns them into progress signals that are comparable, analyzable, and tied to outcomes." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/how-it-works.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "When the student completes the activity, the system records the result, updates mastery state, and feeds signals into the analytics pipeline. ... Apps provide XP based on their own reward logic, and the platform aggregates it across the ecosystem." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Glossary: "Mastery: Demonstrated competence in a learning objective. Tracked by PowerPath." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/build-on-timeback/reference/glossary.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- PowerPath API tag: "Lesson Mastery ... Track and manage student mastery of lesson objectives, through PowerPath 100 lessons" (OpenAPI in https://docs.timeback.com/beta/api-reference/beyond-ai/powerpath/course-sequence/get-a-single-course-sequence-by-id.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Level 1 (existing third-party apps) feature row: "Core learning events (XP, mastery) | ✔ | ✔" (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/build-on-timeback/integration-levels.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "If your app tracks mastery at the lesson level, pass `masteredUnits` and let the server auto-compute `pctComplete` for you." and "`masteredUnits` is an **incremental** count; it represents how many new units (lessons) a student mastered during **this specific activity**, not a cumulative total." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/build-on-timeback/sdk/activity-tracking/course-progress.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "A shared data model helps interoperability. It does not automatically make two apps' identifiers, progress scales, or mastery policies interchangeable." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/how-it-works.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- EduBridge endpoint description: "Gets the highest grade mastered by a student for a given subject across different data sources (currently edulastic, placement and test out datasets)." (listed in https://docs.timeback.com/_llms/beta/api-reference.txt, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Non-negotiables for every app: "Enforce mastery gates at ≥90% accuracy. Do not advance students based on time, completion, or self-report." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/concepts/non-negotiables.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Christensen Institute view: "The outputs of third-party learning software are noisy. Those products were built to deliver their own instruction and report on their own terms, not to hand Timeback a granular picture of what students know and where their misconceptions lie. Alpha can see that a student finished a module in an outside program. It has a much harder time seeing why the student struggled, or what to serve up next." (https://www.christenseninstitute.org/blog/could-alpha-school-disrupt-k-12-education/, accessed 2026-10-24)

## E4 Data protection evidence
- OAuth2 client-credentials scopes in the PowerPath OpenAPI include: "https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/or/v1p2/scope/admin.own: Admin access restricted to own resources", "https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/or/v1p1/scope/roster-core.readonly: Read access to roster core", "https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/or/v1p1/scope/roster-demographics.readonly: Read access to roster demographics", "https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/or/v1p1/scope/gradebook.createput: Create and update access to gradebook", "https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/powerpath/v1p1/scope/powerpath.readonly: Read access to powerpath" (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/api-reference/beyond-ai/powerpath/course-sequence/get-a-hole-filling-app-id.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Send an access token with the `https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/caliper/v1p2/scope/events.write` scope. Use HTTPS and send events from a trusted backend." (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/build-on-timeback/reference/event-contracts.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Standards table, OneRoster row: "Map identities and use supported operations/scopes" (https://docs.timeback.com/beta/about-timeback/how-it-works.md, accessed 2026-10-24)
- EduBridge endpoint description: "Student visibility and tenant isolation are enforced in the same statement as the read." (listed in https://docs.timeback.com/_llms/beta/api-reference.txt, accessed 2026-10-24)
- No page was found stating how a school or parent grants or limits a specific app's access to a given student's data.
