## E4 Data protection evidence
- Google scope list: "The following is a comprehensive listing of the restricted scopes we may request for your students and teachers to access certain features and how we use them. Just so you know, we never request Offline access, as such these permissions are only available while users are actively using Writable." (https://intercom.help/writable/en/articles/8548443-how-do-i-enable-writable-as-a-third-party-app-for-google-access, accessed 2026-10-24)

- "Google Workspace APIs. We do not use Google Workspace APIs or your Personal Information to develop, improve, or train generalized/non-personalized artificial intelligence and/or machine learning (collectively “AI”) models. We may transfer assignment metadata and/or de-identified student writing submissions retrieved via Google Workspace APIs to third-party AI tools to generate scoring and feedback results or perform analyses or other functions required to provide our Service."
