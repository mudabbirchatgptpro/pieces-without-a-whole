## E1 Sequencing / scheduling evidence
- VoyageMath (Wayground math practice): "VoyageMath uses an adaptive algorithm; it analyzes student performance data to personalize learning paths. Each student will receive questions at their optimal level of difficulty, ensuring both challenge and progress." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404963-voyagemath-faqs, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Learners answer questions across various difficulty levels based on their current skill level and work towards mastery." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404959-introducing-voyagemath-, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Currently, VoyageMath skills can only be assigned to students who are part of a Wayground class." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404963-voyagemath-faqs, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Rotating Question Sets: "In each reattempt, students might see any combination of previously unanswered questions, incorrectly answered questions, and correctly answered questions. This allows for practice through spaced repetition while discouraging students from copying each others' answers." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404932-rotating-question-sets, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Here’s how you can enable adaptive learning on your Assessment/Quiz:" (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Mastery Peak: "In the Mastery Peak mode, students aim to achieve an accuracy goal by reattempting incorrectly answered questions." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404926-host-an-assessment-quiz-in-mastery-peak-mode, accessed 2026-10-24)
- AI next steps for teachers: "You can quickly and easily Analyze data from your reports, gain valuable insights into student progress, and get suggestions for the next steps" (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000403991-what-is-wayground-, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Content scope: sequencing documented above operates on Wayground resources (teacher-created, Wayground Library, VoyageMath). No documentation found of sequencing activities from other vendors' tools.

## E4 Data protection evidence
- Wayground AI Chrome extension Google OAuth scopes: "When teachers use Wayground AI, it requests Google permissions scoped to what each feature needs." The scope table lists "Read Google Drive (Docs, Slides, Forms, Drive files)", "Create/manage only files it creates", "Read Classroom courses", "Read Classroom rosters", "Read student submissions", "Write Classroom coursework" (with "The only write access; used to post grades and comments to Classroom, and only when a teacher clicks Apply"), and "There are no Gmail, Calendar, or Contacts scopes ." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000453414-deploy-wayground-ai-for-your-school-or-district, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "If a student account opens the extension, it's automatically blocked with a "For Teachers Only" message." (same URL, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Google Workspace trusted-app control: "Only the Google Workspace for Education admin can mark Wayground as trusted." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404021-add-wayground-as-a-trusted-app-in-google-workspace-for-education, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Google Classroom add-on permissions: "These permissions let the add-on post assignments and sync grades in Google Classroom." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000451635-assign-wayground-resources-in-google-classroom, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Subprocessor table includes a "Processes student data" column, e.g. "Amazon Web Services" ("Cloud services, website hosting, and data center services.", "Yes") and "Braze" ("Email communication system used to send transactional emails and updates to non-student end-users.", "No") (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404023-subprocessors-and-service-providers-used-by-wayground, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Privacy-centered design means that students can use Wayground without creating accounts or sharing PII unless required to use a specific feature such as saving progress to a profile." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404026-how-does-wayground-protect-student-data-, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Additionally, we are proud members of the School Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC) and Access 4 Learning (A4L) Community." (https://help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000404021-add-wayground-as-a-trusted-app-in-google-workspace-for-education, accessed 2026-10-24)
- Note: the scope lists above are scopes Wayground requests from Google; no documentation found of Wayground granting per-app scopes to other tools over its own student data.
