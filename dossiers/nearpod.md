## E3 Learner model evidence
- "Reports are broken into three sections: the summary, the activity report, and the student report. All three types of reports are automatically generated for every Live and Student-Paced lesson, video, or activity you launch in Nearpod. Each report is tied to your unique lesson code or link, capturing a time-stamped record of student participation and responses." (https://nearpod.com/blog/teacher-reports/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "Available on the Nearpod platform, reports can be viewed online or downloaded as a PDF or CSV for further analysis and collaboration." (https://nearpod.com/blog/teacher-reports/, accessed 2026-10-24)
- "When an LMS course is copied, Nearpod will start a new session and the post-session report will only include the new group of students." (https://support.renaissance.com/s/article/LTI-1-3-Canvas-1752689735798?language=en_US, accessed 2026-10-24)
