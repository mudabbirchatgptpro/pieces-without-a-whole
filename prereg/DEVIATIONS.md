# Deviations from the pre-registration

Recorded 24-25 October 2026, after evidence collection and **before any product was coded**.

1. **Stratum 5.4 list.** The most recent Instructure EdTech Top 40 on the registration date is the 2026 edition (school year 2025-26, released 29 June 2026), not the 2025 edition. It counts tools launched inside Canvas through LTI, which favors tools already integrated with Canvas. It is printed alphabetically, with no ranking, so the "trim from the bottom of its ranking" rule cannot be applied. Rather than drop 15 products by alphabet, all 29 instructional entries are coded. The 60-product cap was set for workload, not for inference, and coding more products makes P2 (a universal negative) harder to pass, not easier. Coded total: 67 products (26 + 12 + 29); the 8 AI products of 5.3 are audited for RQ3, not classified.
2. **Stratum 5.1 search.** The search tool has no result pages, so each fixed query was run in standard mode, extended mode, and standard mode with already-returned domains blocked. For "learning operating system" the third pass added "platform K-12 OR students OR schools", because the bare phrase returned only computer science courses. Computer operating systems for devices (for example Windows Education, Endless OS) were excluded as a class: the stratum is products describing themselves as an operating system for learning or schools, not software that runs hardware. Products were included when the vendor's own live site uses "OS" or "operating system" for a school or learning product, including in the product name; a candidate with only an app-store listing and no vendor site was excluded. Full log: research/frame_5_1_search_log.md.
3. **1EdTech terms.** Evidence agents never queried the 1EdTech certification directory. The 1EdTech legal page also bars automated access to any portion of its website, which covers the scripted reading of about 60 public specification pages for the standards evidence. Every quotation used from 1EdTech pages will be re-confirmed by hand, or written permission obtained, before publication.
