## Deviations and flags for the author

1. **Not the 2025 report.** The 2025 report (2024-25 school year, "accessed an average of 2,982 distinct edtech" tools; https://www.instructure.com/sites/default/files/pdf/2025_edtech-top-40-ebook.pdf) has been superseded by the 2026 K-12 edition above. Prereg 5.4 says "most recent ... available on 24 October 2026", so the 2026 edition is used.
2. **Method changed.** The 2026 list ranks Canvas LTI launches, not browser web traffic. Report p. 2: "Instead of tracking general web traffic, this year's list analyzes the most frequently launched Learning Tools Interoperability (LTI) applications in the Instructure ecosystem, specifically in Canvas LMS." This biases the frame toward tools that integrate with Canvas by LTI (relevant to E2).
3. **No published ranking.** The p. 7 list is alphabetical. The report mentions internal ranks only in passing (p. 11: "The only large language model (LLM) on the list is Gemini, which ranks 38th"; SchoolAI "ranks 85th", MagicSchool "ranking at 243"). The 2025 edition is also alphabetical. "Rank order" below is therefore the report's printed (alphabetical) order. The prereg trimming rule ("trimmed from the bottom of its ranking") cannot be applied mechanically; it is not needed because 29 entries qualify (under 30).
4. **A later higher-education edition exists.** "EdTech Top 40: Inside the U.S. higher education digital learning stack" (released 2026-09-29), https://www.instructure.com/resources/research-reports/edtech-top-40-inside-us-higher-education-digital-learning-stack, PDF https://www.instructure.com/sites/default/files/pdf/EDUCAUSE%20ETT40%20Higher%20Ed%20Report-compressed.pdf. It is literally newer, but it is a separate higher-ed series; its list was not extracted (text layer did not yield it). The K-12 edition was used as the direct successor of the series. The author should confirm this reading of 5.4.
