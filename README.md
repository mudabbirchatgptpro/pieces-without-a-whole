# Pieces Without a Whole

Open materials for a study by Mudaber Jamshaid testing Christensen's modularity theory against education technology and AI.

- `prereg/PREREGISTRATION.md`: research questions, predictions with refutation criteria, codebook and sampling frame, committed before any product was coded.
- Data, dossiers, codes and the paper are added in later commits. The commit history is the record of what was fixed when.
